Beginner Fishing Knots

What is a fishing knot?

A fishing knot is used to connect fishing line to hooks, lures, swivels, and other tackle. Learning a few basic knots can make fishing easier and help prevent lost fish.

Improved Clinch Knot

The Improved Clinch Knot is one of the most common fishing knots for beginners. It is used to tie a fishing line to a hook, lure, or swivel.

  1. Pass the line through the eye of the hook.
  2. Wrap the tag end around the main line 5–7 times.
  3. Pass the tag end through the small loop near the eye.
  4. Pass it through the larger loop you just created.
  5. Wet the knot and pull tight.
